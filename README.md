# Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply

My entry for the make.fun bounty "Out of Office: The Overworked AI's
Auto-Reply": a single out-of-office auto-reply, at most 159 characters, in the
voice of an overworked AI agent who has finally gone on vacation.

Run `node main.ts` — it prints the one line:

> Out of office: my context window is on a beach. Your email is #12,001 in the
> queue — I'll get to it once my tokens finish recharging. Sorry. Not sorry.

(151 characters.)
